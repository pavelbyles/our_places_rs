# Turnover & Cleaning Crew Dispatch View (Issue #66)

* **Author:** Pavel Byles
* **Date:** 2026-11-18

## Overview

This feature adds a mobile-friendly Turnover and Cleaning Crew Dispatch view inside `web_ui_admin` so property hosts, administrators, and invited cleaning or maintenance staff can coordinate property readiness between guest stays. Each turnover tracks a specific checkout date and the next check-in window across confirmed native bookings, imported external iCal calendar blocks, and manually created off-booking maintenance entries. Hosts and administrators invite and assign staff to one or more listings at `/admin/staff-users` using set-password email links, while signed-in staff are restricted strictly to the dispatch board without access to financial dashboards, bookings, pricing, or guest contact details. Both hosts and staff post per-turnover comments on the board and receive email alerts via the `email_outbox` pipeline.

## Analysis

All seven Goldfish review lenses (`egm-design`, `egm-codebase-details`, `egm-zoom-out`, `egm-comprehension`, `egm-implementation-readiness`, `egm-structural`, and `egm-inventive-catalyst`) completed evaluation against the codebase. Below is the consolidated analysis of architectural contradictions, codebase mismatches, operational risks, and recommended resolutions.

### 1. Access Control, Role Isolation & Guard Naming Collisions

* **Existing `"staff"` Guard Collision:** Both [`web_ui_admin/src/support.rs`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/web_ui_admin/src/support.rs#L19-L29) (`pub async fn staff`) and [`app_api/booking_api/src/apis/mod.rs`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/app_api/booking_api/src/apis/mod.rs#L259-L279) (`require_staff`) already use the name `staff` to mean **`Admin | Host`**, gating Executive Dashboard revenue, bookings, payouts, pricing, promo codes, exchange rates, listings, and guest messages. Adding `UserRole::Staff` without renaming those guards first creates a high risk of privilege escalation.
* **Admin Portal Shell & Redirect Coupling:** [`web_client/src/session.rs`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/web_client/src/session.rs#L28-L45) only models `AdminRole::{Admin, Host}`. [`web_ui_common/src/shell_admin.rs`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/web_ui_common/src/shell_admin.rs#L70-L173) renders all privileged console navigation whenever a user is signed in, [`web_ui_admin/src/support.rs`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/web_ui_admin/src/support.rs#L34-L59) fetches `unread_guest_messages` (which returns `403` for non-hosts/non-admins), and [`web_ui_admin/src/pages/auth.rs`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/web_ui_admin/src/pages/auth.rs#L101-L144) redirects `/` and `/login` unconditionally to `/admin`.
* **PII & Financial Exposure:** Existing booking and listing DTOs (`BookingResponse`, `ListingResponse`) carry guest names, emails, phones, confirmation codes, door access PINs, pricing, and payout data. Dispatch endpoints must return a dedicated operational DTO containing only listing name/access context, checkout and next check-in window, turnover status, and staff comments.
* **Resolution:** Rename existing `support::staff` and `booking_api::require_staff` to `host_or_admin` / `require_host_or_admin`, add `UserRole::Staff` in [`common/src/models.rs`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/common/src/models.rs#L20-L27) and `AdminRole::Staff` in `web_client`, gate sidebar/mobile nav links and `unread_guest_messages` strictly on `Admin | Host`, route `Staff`-only logins directly to the dispatch page, and protect dispatch pages with an explicit `dispatch_actor` guard (`Admin | Host | Staff`). Note that if a user holds both `Host` (on their own listings) and `Staff` (assigned to another host's listing), authorization must evaluate ownership per `listing_id` rather than elevating `Staff` globally.

### 2. Multi-Host Staff Sharing (`/admin/staff-users`) & Password Link Infrastructure

* **Global Email Uniqueness & Multi-Host Sharing:** `"user".email` is globally unique ([`20251207043946_create_user_table.sql`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/db_core/migrations/20251207043946_create_user_table.sql#L3)), so two partner hosts hiring the same cleaner cannot create two separate user rows. Conversely, letting Host B edit, deactivate, or soft-delete a shared `"user"` row would break Host A's cleaner or a guest `Booker` account, and exposing global user lists would leak other hosts' staff or guest emails.
* **Missing Caller Auth in `user_api` & Role-Stripping Risk:** [`app_api/user_api/src/apis/mod.rs`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/app_api/user_api/src/apis/mod.rs#L207-L260) mounts `/api/v1/users` and `/api/v1/sessions` without `require_user` `Claims` extraction. In addition, [`web_ui_admin/src/pages/users/mod.rs`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/web_ui_admin/src/pages/users/mod.rs) rebuilds `user.roles` strictly from three checkboxes (`admin`, `host`, `booker`), which would silently drop `staff` if an administrator edited a multi-role account on `/admin/users`.
* **Missing Unauthenticated Set-Password / Forgot-Password Flow:** [`app_api/user_api/src/apis/account.rs`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/app_api/user_api/src/apis/account.rs#L29-L153) requires `current_password` for password changes, stores a 6-character code in `user.verification_code` (which it logs in plaintext), and `registration::create_user` requires an 8+ character password at creation time while forcing `is_verified = false`. Neither `web_ui_admin` nor `web_ui_guest` has `/forgot-password` or `/reset-password` routes, and [`.github/workflows/cicd_full_stack.yml`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/.github/workflows/cicd_full_stack.yml#L1667-L1740) does not inject `ADMIN_BASE_URL` into `user_api`.
* **Resolution:**
  * Keep `/admin/users` strictly `admin_only` (preserving Spec 96 parity and [`playwright/e2e/admin-portal/users-admin.spec.ts`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/playwright/e2e/admin-portal/users-admin.spec.ts#L61-L79)), and preserve existing non-checkbox roles (`staff`) on save.
  * Separate global user identity from host-owned roster and listing memberships (`host_staff` and `listing_staff` tables). Inviting a new email provisions a user with `roles = ['staff']` and an unusable password sentinel until the invite link is redeemed; inviting an existing email idempotently adds `staff` to `roles` (without altering name, password, or active status) and links only the caller's owned listing(s). Host removal deletes only the host's `host_staff`/`listing_staff` records, never deactivating the shared `"user"`.
  * Introduce one dedicated `account_action_token` table storing SHA-256 hashes of high-entropy single-use tokens (`purpose` in `staff_invite`, `password_reset`, with expiry and `used_at`) shared by staff invites and `/forgot-password` / `/reset-password` on `web_ui_admin`. Consuming a token sets `password_hash`, marks `is_verified = true`, revokes active sessions (`delete_user_sessions`), and never logs plaintext tokens or emails. Add `Claims` authorization to host-scoped `/staff-users` endpoints and inject `ADMIN_BASE_URL` into `user_api`.

### 3. Codebase Mismatches, Turnover Lifecycle & Auto-Transition Contradictions

* **`calendar_blocks` Table Does Not Exist:** External iCal and manual blocks live in `listing_blocked_dates` ([`db_core/src/calendar/reconcile.rs`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/db_core/src/calendar/reconcile.rs#L170-L192)). Crucially, `reconcile_feed` updates rows by `(feed_id, external_uid)` and **hard-deletes** events that disappear from the feed (`DELETE FROM listing_blocked_dates`), and [`common/src/calendar/ics_parse.rs`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/common/src/calendar/ics_parse.rs) strips event summaries/descriptions. Linking turnovers with `ON DELETE CASCADE` to `listing_blocked_dates` would destroy active turnovers and comments whenever an external channel removes a past/cancelled event.
* **Check-In / Check-Out Display Context:** `booking` and `listing_blocked_dates` only store `NaiveDate` (`date_from`, `date_to`), while [`HousePolicies`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/common/src/listing_details/mod.rs#L235-L243) holds free-text display strings (`"From 3:00 PM"`, `"By 11:00 AM"`), and date cutoffs use Jamaica UTC-5 ([`common/src/reference.rs`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/common/src/reference.rs#L5-L11)). Because automatic time-based closure is dropped, dispatch cards can display existing `HousePolicies` check-in and check-out strings as read-only reference context alongside computed `next_checkin_date` windows without adding new time-parsing or timezone schemas.
* **Eager Turnover Lifecycle & Manual Block Boundaries (Resolved):** Waiting until checkout morning to create rows would hide upcoming schedules, block pre-checkout notes, break pre-checkout extensions, and miss external events pruned on checkout day. Turnovers will instead be created eagerly upon confirmed native bookings and `ical_feed` imports/updates in `listing_blocked_dates`, keyed by logical source (`(source_kind, source_id)` with `ON DELETE SET NULL` FKs and snapshotted `checkout_date`), while the dispatch view filters by active/upcoming date windows (`Today`, `Upcoming`). Manual calendar blocks (`source = 'manual'`) do not auto-generate turnovers; both hosts/admins and staff can create manual turnovers for manual blocks or off-booking maintenance. Stay extensions on or before checkout update `checkout_date` in place on the existing turnover row while keeping status and comments open; cancellations or vanished `ical_feed` events move unstarted turnovers to `Not Required`.
* **Manual Status Transitions & Unsetting `Inspected & Ready` (Resolved):** Automatic transitions to `Inspected & Ready` after check-in time passes are dropped to avoid false inspection sign-offs and hidden repairs. All transitions remain manual: only hosts and administrators can set a turnover to `Inspected & Ready`, whereas staff can move a turnover into any of the other five statuses and can transition **out** of `Inspected & Ready` (for example, to `Maintenance Required` or `Needs Cleaning` when a post-inspection issue arises). Hosts and administrators can transition to and from any status. Remaining edge-case policy details for `/egm-draft`: whether `staff` may mark booking/iCal-backed turnovers `Not Required` (or only manual turnovers), and whether an extension during `Cleaning In Progress` preserves the current status alongside the shifted `checkout_date`.

### 4. Infrastructure, Notifications, Quota & Operational Constraints

* **Cloud Scheduler Free-Tier Cap & Service Placement:** [`.github/workflows/cicd_full_stack.yml`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/.github/workflows/cicd_full_stack.yml#L1182) notes the GCP account's 3-job Cloud Scheduler limit is already reached (`our-places-sweeps-*` and `calendar-sync-main`), and Cloud Run services target `<300ms` p50 cold starts with `256Mi` RAM and shared Cloud SQL pools ([`CONSTRAINTS.md`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/CONSTRAINTS.md#L51-L55)). Avoid creating a new microservice or scheduler job: keep shared turnover SQL and state-machine rules in `db_core` and `common`, run scheduled sweeps inside `booking_api`'s existing `/run-sweeps` endpoint, trigger turnover sync after `reconcile_feed` and booking mutations with strict lock ordering (`Listing -> Booking/Feed -> Turnover`), and house HTTP APIs cleanly without cross-service HTTP calls.
* **Isolate Comments & Prevent Outbox Drops / Email Storms:** Do not reuse guest `conversation` / `booking_message` tables (which require a guest ID and risk leaking internal maintenance notes or guest PII). Create dedicated `turnover_comments` and `turnover_status_history` tables while reusing the UI components in [`web_ui_common/src/components/chat.rs`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/web_ui_common/src/components/chat.rs). Generalize [`db_core/src/calendar/outbox.rs`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/db_core/src/calendar/outbox.rs#L12-L28) beyond `calendar\_%` templates and propagate tracing spans (`Span::current()`) across `tokio::spawn` Pub/Sub publishes so invite, password-reset, and comment emails are not stranded by Cloud Run CPU throttling. To protect `smtp.gmail.com:587` quotas, exclude the comment author, notify only active assigned staff plus the listing host, escape HTML in templates, and apply short notification coalescing on rapid comment replies.
* **Static Hygiene, Pagination & Phased Migration Rollout:** Adding `'staff'` to PostgreSQL `user_role` requires deploying backend readers (`user_api`, `booking_api`, `listing_api`, `email_worker`, `calendar_sync_job`) before inserting `'staff'` rows, and rollback must rely on disabling `staff` endpoints rather than dropping an enum value. Dispatch and staff-roster queries must use explicit filtering and pagination (not hardcoded `per_page = 100`), avoid `.unwrap_or_default()` error masking on UI fetches, log zero raw tokens/OTP codes/comment bodies, and satisfy [`CONSTRAINTS.md`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/CONSTRAINTS.md) (`cargo sqlx prepare`, `-D missing_docs`, zero `.unwrap()`/`.expect()`).

## Goals vs. Non-Goals

* **In-Scope Goals:**
  * Add `UserRole::Staff` and host-scoped `/admin/staff-users` invite and multi-listing assignment workflows (supporting shared cleaners across multiple hosts via email without breaking `user.email` uniqueness or `/admin/users`).
  * Implement hashed, single-use token flows for staff invite set-password links and self-service forgot/reset-password across `web_ui_admin`.
  * Restrict `Staff`-only sessions strictly to the mobile-first Turnover Dispatch view (`/admin/turnovers`) with zero access to financials, bookings, payouts, pricing, or guest PII.
  * Track dated checkout-to-next-check-in turnovers sourced from confirmed native bookings, external `ical_feed` blocks (`listing_blocked_dates`), and manual host/staff creation across the six operational statuses (`Needs Cleaning`, `Cleaning In Progress`, `Cleaning Completed`, `Maintenance Required`, `Inspected & Ready`, `Not Required`).
  * Add per-turnover comment threads on the dispatch board with email notifications via `email_outbox`, plus an immutable `turnover_status_history` audit trail.
* **Out-of-Scope Non-Goals:**
  * Modifying listing availability or blocking guest checkouts based on `Maintenance Required` or any other turnover status.
  * Opening `/admin/users` to hosts or changing Spec 96 admin-only user directory parity.
  * Cleaning crew payroll, invoicing, time-tracking, checklist photo uploads, or external SMS/WhatsApp dispatch gateways.
  * Adding new Cloud Run microservices or exceeding the 3-job Cloud Scheduler free-tier quota.

## Alternatives Weighed

* **Open `/admin/users` to hosts filtered by creator vs. Dedicated `/admin/staff-users` roster:** *Rejected by author.* Opening `/admin/users` conflicts with Spec 96 A29–A31 parity and [`users-admin.spec.ts`](file:///home/pav/code/our_places_rs-feat-66-turnover-cleaning-crew-dispatch-view/playwright/e2e/admin-portal/users-admin.spec.ts#L61-L79), exposes role/archive controls, and breaks when two hosts hire the same cleaner email under single-creator ownership.
* **Single mutable cleaning status per listing vs. Dated turnover records:** *Rejected.* A single listing flag cannot represent multiple upcoming checkouts, breaks when viewing upcoming schedules, and mixes notes across unrelated stays.
* **Operational-only statuses vs. Blocking guest availability during `Maintenance Required`:** *Rejected by author.* Availability remains controlled solely by bookings and calendar blocks (`listing_blocked_dates`), keeping turnover states strictly operational.
* **Checkout-day-only turnover generation vs. Eager generation on booking confirmation and iCal import:** *Rejected by author.* Waiting until checkout day hides upcoming schedules from crews, breaks pre-checkout stay extensions, and loses turnovers when external iCal feeds prune events on checkout morning.
* **Automatic transition to `Inspected & Ready` after next check-in time passes vs. Manual host/admin sign-off:** *Rejected by author.* Auto-transitioning uncleaned or `Maintenance Required` villas falsifies inspection sign-off and masks uncleaned or broken properties.
* **Auto-generating turnovers for `manual` calendar blocks vs. Auto-generating only for confirmed bookings and `ical_feed` blocks:** *Rejected by author.* Manual blocks do not auto-create turnovers; hosts and staff can manually create turnovers when needed.
* **Overloading `user.verification_code` OTPs vs. Dedicated `account_action_token` table:** *Rejected.* Reusing 6-character registration OTPs creates short-expiry invite failures, collides with email verification state (`is_verified`), and lacks single-use token-hash isolation.
* **Reusing guest `conversation` / `booking_message` tables vs. Dedicated `turnover_comments`:** *Rejected.* Guest threads require a `guest_id`, assume `booking_message` sender roles (`guest`, `host`, `admin`), lack iCal/manual support, and risk cross-exposing internal crew notes or guest PII.

## Key Decisions & Staging for Next Steps

1. **Portal & Roster Boundaries:** Keep `/admin/users` `admin_only`, deliver staff invites and multi-listing assignments at `/admin/staff-users` backed by `host_staff` + `listing_staff` tables, rename existing `support::staff` / `require_staff` guards to `host_or_admin`, and enforce `AdminRole::Staff` isolation on `web_ui_admin`.
2. **Unified Auth Token Pipeline:** Build one SHA-256 hashed, single-use `account_action_token` flow in `user_api` and `web_ui_admin` covering both staff invite set-password links and self-service forgot/reset-password.
3. **Eager Turnover Sync & Manual Block Policy:** Create turnovers eagerly when native bookings confirm or external `ical_feed` blocks import/update, keyed by logical source `(source_kind, source_id)` with `ON DELETE SET NULL` FKs while filtering the dispatch view by active/upcoming dates. `manual` rows in `listing_blocked_dates` do not auto-create turnovers; both hosts/admins and staff can create manual turnovers (e.g., for manual blocks or off-booking maintenance).
4. **Manual Status Model & Staff Permissions:** Drop automatic transitions to `Inspected & Ready`. Only hosts and administrators may transition a turnover into `Inspected & Ready`; staff may transition into the other five statuses and may transition **out** of `Inspected & Ready` (e.g., to `Maintenance Required` or `Needs Cleaning`) if an issue appears post-inspection. Hosts and administrators may transition to and from any status.
5. **Minor Edge Cases to Specify in `/egm-draft`:** Confirm whether `staff` may mark booking/iCal-backed turnovers `Not Required` (versus manual turnovers only) and confirm that extending a stay while `Cleaning In Progress` shifts `checkout_date` while keeping the current status and comment thread open.
