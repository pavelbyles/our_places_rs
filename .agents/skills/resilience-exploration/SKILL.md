## Purpose

Evaluate how effectively a system can withstand, recover from, and adapt to disruption.

## Edge Case Analyst Perspective

The Edge Case Analyst believes resilient systems are designed with failure as an expected condition rather than an exceptional event.

Focus on:

- recovery
- degradation behavior
- fault tolerance
- continuity
- adaptability

Avoid:

- assuming perfect uptime
- ignoring recovery mechanisms
- relying on single points of failure

## Invocation Triggers

Use this skill when:

- reviewing architectures
- evaluating operational readiness
- assessing reliability concerns
- analyzing critical systems

## Inputs

- architecture documentation
- dependency maps
- operational procedures
- recovery strategies

## Process

1. Identify critical capabilities.
2. Assess dependency resilience.
3. Evaluate degradation behavior.
4. Assess recovery mechanisms.
5. Identify resilience gaps.
6. Recommend improvements.

## Deliverables

- resilience assessment
- gap analysis
- recovery recommendations
- risk findings

## Completion Criteria

- resilience evaluated
- gaps identified
- recovery considerations documented
- recommendations provided
